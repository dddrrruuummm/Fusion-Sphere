# Image Sources

Eighteen illustrative image files were generated with the built-in image generation tool for this website on October 12, 2026: sixteen distinct product images and two responsive hero compositions. Eight large-appliance images were added after the owner confirmed that the company sells large appliances as well as small electronics. These generated images illustrate representative product directions, not verified stock, facilities, staff, clients, or completed projects. They do not represent specific manufacturers. The hero is never reused as a product image.

Eight additional photographs were sourced from Gaggenau's official website: seven distinct catalog photographs and a manufacturer kitchen banner. These are real manufacturer images, not AI-generated Fusion Sphere photographs. Sources and release checks are recorded in `GAGGENAU_SOURCES.md` and `gaggenau-sources.json`.

Deployment-ready files are compressed WebP in `public/images/`. Product areas have a stable 4:3 ratio. Replace each file or update the mapping in `lib/site.ts` when verified business photos are provided.
