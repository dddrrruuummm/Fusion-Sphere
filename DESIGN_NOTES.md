# Fusion Sphere: two worlds, one source

The company has two commercially distinct product fields: electrical/electronic goods and clothing/accessories. The brand uses two intersecting half-spheres as a compact custom mark, cobalt blue as the central anchor, lemon yellow as a transition surface, and coral only through product imagery. Bold sans-serif typography replaces the serif editorial language used by KKL and JENNIFERFY.

Signature elements: an electronics/apparel studio still life, an intersecting-sphere logo, a two-sector category band, and a product brief organized around product/scope/destination.

References read: Nike for photography-led brand presence; Apple for precise product presentation; IBM for legible B2B information. The site has its own colors, geometry, logo, copy, section order, and product content.

All product images retain 4:3 geometry across mobile and desktop. Hero photography is separate from catalog assets. Controls are fixed dimensions and keyboard accessible. Reduced-motion settings disable transitions.

Generic category images were generated using the built-in image generation tool. Full prompts and asset mapping are recorded in ASSETS.md. Gaggenau uses separate official manufacturer photographs, a dedicated full-width kitchen banner, and a brand catalog filter; sources are recorded in GAGGENAU_SOURCES.md. Neither image set is evidence of current Fusion Sphere inventory. The shared wordmark displays Fusion Sphere with capital F and S in both header and footer.
